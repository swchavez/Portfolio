My career began at the intersection of data and strategy, leveraging my B.A. in Public Policy Leadership to drive operations as a Data Director for statewide political campaigns. Recognizing the power of technical infrastructure, I earned an M.B.A. with a focus on web analytics. This catalyzed my pivot into the FinTech space, where I now serve as a Senior Product Manager, leading development teams and managing complex appraisal order and collateral quality control platforms.

Beyond the product roadmap, my foundation is built on family. I met my wife, Mary Kathryn, during our time at Ole Miss, and we were married in Oxford in 2022. Our lives are kept busy and grounded by our toy poodle, Moose, and our first son, who we welcomed in May 2026. Whether I am analyzing market trends, building technical prototypes, or spending time with my family, my focus remains on creating scalable, meaningful impact.
